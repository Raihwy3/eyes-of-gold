Eyes of Gold Launcher - credits

Engine and port

GoldenEye decompilation contributors, and James Dansereau and contributors
for the GoldenEye PC port on which this project is built.
https://github.com/jkdansereau/goldeneye-pc-port
Their original notices and the Fast3D renderer license are included unchanged.

Controls

Julian "Jibb" Smart and contributors: GamepadMotionHelpers for gyro processing,
orientation spaces and calibration, and JoyShockMapper for the adapted
flick-stick timing and rotation smoothing.
https://github.com/JibbSmart/GamepadMotionHelpers
https://github.com/JibbSmart/JoyShockMapper

3D

OneUp (oneup03) and RT64 contributors: the dynamic3d guidance and rt64-3ds
stereo implementation used for convergence, depth sampling and reticle depth.
https://github.com/oneup03/3D-Skills/tree/main/dynamic3d
https://github.com/oneup03/rt64-3ds
The port adapts parts of this work; it does not include the complete RT64 renderer.

Thanks

IDrinkLava on GBAtemp for the gyro feedback and for pointing us toward
JibbSmart's GamepadMotionHelpers and the DK64 port's motion-control approach.

OneUp on GBAtemp for pointing us toward the dynamic3d guidance and rt64-3ds
stereo implementation.
