Eyes of Gold Launcher

Installation

1. Copy the included 3ds folder to the root of your SD card.
2. Put your legally backed-up US retail GoldenEye 007 ROM in:
   /3ds/eyes-of-gold-native-game/
   Name it GoldenEye.n64, GoldenEye.z64 or GoldenEye.v64.
3. Open Eyes of Gold Launcher through the Homebrew Launcher.

The first launch prepares the game files on your console. Leave at least
48 MiB free on the SD card and let setup finish. Later launches reuse the
prepared files. Keep the ROM in the folder for updates or repairs.

All 20 campaign missions are available. The multiplayer maps are an
experimental preview with one controlled player and a second idle player.
There is no networking yet. Bugs and performance drops are still possible,
especially on Old 3DS systems.
