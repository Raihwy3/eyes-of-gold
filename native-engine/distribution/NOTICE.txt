Eyes of Gold Launcher

This is an unofficial 3DS port of the GoldenEye 007 engine, built on the
GoldenEye decompilation and PC port. It adds a 3DS platform layer, rendering,
controls and menu changes.

You need your own supported US retail ROM. The launcher checks it and prepares
the game files on your SD card. The install ZIP does not include a ROM,
extracted game assets or generated asset caches. The executable does contain
compiled engine code, conversion code and the metadata needed to read the ROM.

All 20 campaign missions are enabled. Multiplayer is an experimental preview
of 11 arenas, with one controlled player and a second idle player.
